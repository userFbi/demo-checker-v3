Before you order

Quick answers

Timelines, prices and shipping, stated plainly.

Still have questions? Contact the team
How long does stock production take?
5–7 business days for normal production. With a rush fee we can produce and ship in 2–3 business days.
Can my graphics be etched into a stock chain?
No. On stock chains, graphics are applied as vinyl on top of the metal.
How long does a fully custom premier chain take?
Normally 3–4 weeks after your artwork is approved and the deposit is received. The deposit is half of the invoice.
How much does a fully custom premier chain cost?
Most range from $450 to $1,300 depending on the options. We can email you a price guide.
How much is shipping?
It depends on the destination. UPS Ground with insurance can be as low as $16.99 from our Ohio location. We also ship internationally, so contact us for prices.
